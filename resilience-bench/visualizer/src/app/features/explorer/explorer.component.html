How the score is calculated

Score = Checkout Success Rate − p95 Iteration Duration

Higher scores are better. The heuristic uses this score to compare configurations and guide the search. Because the success rate is at most 1 and duration is non-negative, the theoretical maximum score is 1; scores can be negative when duration is high.
